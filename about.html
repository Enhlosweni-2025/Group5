About Mthethweni Farming

Pure Production, Shared Prosperity

Dedicated to delivering fresh poultry protein, sustainable agricultural practices, and community development across KwaZulu-Natal.

Our History

Growing Quality. Building Communities.

Founded in 2022, Mthethweni Farming is a proudly South African poultry farm based in Mbongolwane, Eshowe. We specialize in raising healthy broiler chickens while supporting food security, creating local employment, and empowering communities through sustainable farming practices and agricultural mentorship.

Our operations were inspired by the vital need for food security in rural South Africa. Every family deserves access to fresh, affordable protein. Through careful management and local partnerships, we aim to be a pillar of economic strength and food stability.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading poultry producers, recognized for premium quality products, innovation, sustainable farming, and rural youth empowerment.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, fresh protein to rural and urban communities while creating jobs and training aspiring local farmers through structured mentorship.

Quality Assurance

How We Operate

Our multi-step operational workflow guarantees healthy broilers, biological security, and maximum freshness.

1. Raising With Care

Our broilers are raised for 5–6 weeks in clean, ventilated poultry houses using a balanced commercial feeding program supporting healthy growth.

2. Hygiene & Care

Strict biosecurity, thorough disinfection between batches, clean water filtration, and full protective equipment for all farm technicians.

3. Processing & Packaging

Hygienically processed, accurately weighed, packaged in food-grade wrapping, and chilled immediately for optimal preservation.

4. Delivery & Collection

Fresh poultry delivered straight to local buyers, stores, caterers, or available for direct collection at our Mbongolwane farm.
