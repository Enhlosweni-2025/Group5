Vision

Founded in 2022, Mthethweni Farming is a proudly South African poultry farm based in Mbongolwane, Eshowe. We specialize in raising healthy broiler chickens while supporting food security, creating local employment, and empowering communities through sustainable farming practices and agricultural mentorship.

Mission

2. Our Mission / Impact Impact: We want to provide affordable, fresh protein to our rural community and create jobs for local people in Mbongolwane. Inspiration: We are inspired by food security in our area. Every family deserves access to fresh, affordable chicken.

How We Do It

Raising with Care Our broilers are raised for 5–6 weeks in clean, ventilated poultry houses using a balanced commercial feeding programme that supports healthy and consistent growth.

Hygiene & Animal Care

We maintain strict hygiene standards by disinfecting poultry houses between batches, providing fresh feed and clean water daily, and ensuring staff follow proper sanitation and protective clothing procedures.

Processing & Packaging

Before reaching our customers, birds are processed in a hygienic environment, weighed accurately, packaged safely in food-grade packaging, and chilled to maintain freshness. Live broilers are also available by pre-order.

Delivery & Customer Satisfaction

Customers receive fresh, quality poultry products through farm collections, local deliveries, or bulk supply services for businesses and community events.
