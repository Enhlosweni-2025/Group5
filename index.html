About Us

Growing Quality. Building Communities.

Founded in 2022, Mthethweni Farming is a proudly South African poultry farm based in Mbongolwane, Eshowe. We specialize in raising healthy broiler chickens while supporting food security, creating employment opportunities, and empowering communities through sustainable farming and agricultural mentorship.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading poultry producers, recognised for quality products, sustainable farming, innovation, and community development.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, fresh poultry products while creating employment, supporting local food security, and empowering aspiring farmers through mentorship.
